# CURRICULUM.md

## Module 1: The "Scare" (Sensors & Physical Mechanics)
**Concept:** How do machines "feel" when something is wrong?

**Hands-On Activity:** Building simple vibration and temperature sensor circuits using breadboards and LEDs.

**Learning Goal:** Students learn basic electronics, reading analog vs. digital signals, and understanding how real-world rail cars monitor wheel bearings, frame stress, and heat anomalies.

---

## Module 2: The "Scarab" (Micro-Processing & Embedded Logic)
**Concept:** How do small computers work together like a hive?

**Hands-On Activity:** Programming low-cost microcontrollers (e.g., Raspberry Pi Pico / ESP32) to process micro-packets of data.

**Learning Goal:** Introduction to block-based or Python coding. Students learn how microprocessors break big problems into small math packets to solve them locally without relying on the cloud.

---

## Module 3: Signals & Community Mesh (RF & Emergency Alerts)
**Concept:** How do devices talk to each other without the internet or cell towers?

**Hands-On Activity:** Setting up local radio-frequency (RF) or Bluetooth beacons that trigger visual indicator lights when another device comes close.

**Learning Goal:** Understanding wave propagation, signal range, and network priority—mapping directly to real-world emergency broadcasts, health alerts, and community notification systems (Call for the Saints / Call for the Rain Dancers).

---

## Module 4: Enclosures, Fabrication & Silicon Security
**Concept:** Protecting the hardware and locking out backdoors.

**Hands-On Activity:** Designing weather-resistant 3D-printed enclosure models (the "Exoskeleton" and "Elytra") and learning basic physical security concepts.

**Learning Goal:** Introduction to CAD modeling, mechanical assembly, and basic cyber-security principles (hardware keys vs. software backdoors).
